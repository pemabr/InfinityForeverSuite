Guild Ladder DKP 1.4.0
DKP ledger, loot queue, auctions and raid awards for guilds. Works on WoW TBC (20506) and WoW Forever (16001).

Quick start
  /dkp              open the window          /dkp gear       gear plan (BiS targets per slot)
  /dkp fame         Hall of Fame             /dkp raidloot   upgrades from a raid, per boss
  /dkp recap        your season recap        /dkp bis        BiS status, spec and tooltip options
  /dkp reveal       loot reveal banner       /dkp practice   practice mode (nothing is recorded)
  /dkp dock         loot tracker on / off    (small strip with the items you hold and their trade timers)
  /dkp trade        auto trade options       (opens the winner's trade and fills it; you click Trade)
  /dkp enchants     Enchants                 (your enchants against the BiS list; hover one for its reagents)
  /dkp gems         Gems                     (your gems and empty sockets by name, BiS gems by socket colour)

Gear plan, Enchants and Gems also sit in the strip under the main window.

BiS lists, raid drops and tier-token links (TBC only) come from Loon Best In Slot, which compiles Wowhead's TBC class guides.
On WoW Forever those features switch themselves off; the rest of the add-on works as normal.
Window artwork was created for this add-on.

The two data files BiSData_TBC.lua and BiSSources_TBC.lua can be deleted (with their two lines in GuildLadderDKP.toc)
without breaking anything else; the BiS features then stay hidden.
